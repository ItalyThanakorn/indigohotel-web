# Content Clarity Audit

Reviewed against the supplied hotel information and the visible Thai/English copy on all four pages. No missing value has been guessed or presented as a hotel fact.

## Already stated clearly on the site

- Five room names, guest capacity and bed layout; room bed dimensions are listed on the Rooms page.
- Complimentary minibar and kettle in every room; complimentary capsule coffee and Dyson dryer limited to Business, Triple and Deluxe rooms.
- 55-inch Smart TV with Netflix support, in-room facilities, parking locations, 24-hour reception and the complimentary tuk-tuk service within 1 km.
- Address and nearby landmarks, map link and phone/email/messaging/social contact links.
- Check-in/out times, registration documents, cash payment at check-in and key-card deposit/refund instructions.
- Smoking/cannabis/e-cigarette restriction, hair-dye/damage responsibility, and food/pet restrictions.

## Confirm with the hotel before publishing

These are ambiguities in the source, so the website preserves the stated value and this list records what must be clarified.

| Topic | Missing or ambiguous detail | Why guests may misunderstand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Late check-out | The source says check-out by 12:00, a 200 baht hourly fee starts at 13:00, and more than 3 hours is charged as one day. Confirm the grace period and whether the full-day threshold is 15:00, after three charged hours, or another time. | Guests cannot calculate the amount due from departure time. |
| Smoking charge | The source states `5,000` but gives no currency. To avoid publishing an ambiguous charge, the page lists the restriction and asks guests to contact the hotel for the amount/currency. | Guests may interpret the number as baht or another currency. Add it back only after the hotel confirms its currency. |
| Key-card deposit | The source lists 500 baht or 50 ringgit per room. Confirm accepted currency, whether it is cash-only, and whether every room has the same deposit. | The check-in copy says payment is cash-only, but does not say whether that includes the deposit or whether ringgit is accepted in cash. |
| Parking | Cost, capacity, security, reservation requirements and whether spaces are guaranteed are unspecified. | “Parking available” can be mistaken for free or guaranteed parking. |
| Tuk-tuk | How to request the service, operating hours, passenger capacity, and how the 1 km boundary is measured are unspecified. | Guests may assume the service is always available or covers a particular trip. |
| Minibar | Included items and refill policy are unspecified. | “Complimentary minibar” can be read as all items or unlimited refills being free. |
| Netflix | The source says Smart TV supports Netflix, but does not say whether the hotel provides an account or guests must sign in with their own. | Guests may expect an included subscription. |
| Room details | Room sizes, windows/views, extra-bed availability, child policy, occupancy rules for children, and accessible-room features are not supplied. | Guests may need these details to select a room or confirm accessibility. |
| Other guest services | Wi-Fi, breakfast, luggage storage, laundry service, early check-in and late-arrival instructions are not specified. | These are common planning questions, but should not be implied without confirmation. |
| Address | Postal code and the hotel's preferred English address spelling are not supplied. | Guests may need a full address for forms, taxis or delivery. |
| Contact hours | 24-hour front-desk service is stated, but response hours for phone, LINE, email and social channels are not. | Guests may assume every channel is monitored around the clock. |
| Official identity and external links | Confirm the official public display name and that Maps, LINE, WhatsApp and social links still reach hotel-owned destinations. | The logo, text brand name and external profiles may use different naming or outdated links. |
| English policies | Have the hotel approve the English wording for fees, deposits, restrictions and damage responsibility. | A translation can be understandable yet alter a policy's legal or operational meaning. |

## Intentionally not displayed as current availability

Room counts were supplied in the source but are not shown on the website. If the hotel wants to publish totals, label them explicitly as the hotel's total inventory by room type and state that they are not live availability.

## Items intentionally outside this front-end scope

Prices, room availability, reservation confirmation, payment collection and booking-system behavior are not included. The site directs guests to contact the hotel for room inquiries. Do not add these details without confirmed data and a separate implementation decision.
