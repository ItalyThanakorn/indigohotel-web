# User Flows

## Browse in a preferred language

1. Open any page; Thai or English is selected from the saved preference, falling back to the browser language.
2. Choose TH or EN in the header.
3. Page text, image alternative text, document language, page title and description update; the choice remains when navigating between pages.

## Explore rooms

1. Start at Home and choose Rooms, or use the main navigation.
2. Review each of the five room types, guest capacity, bed arrangement and supplied in-room amenities.
3. Open a room photo in the gallery dialog and move through the related photos with the dialog controls.
4. Use a contact action to ask the hotel. The site does not confirm availability or create a reservation.

## Find the hotel or contact the team

1. Open Location & Information from the main navigation or a home-page action.
2. Review the address and nearby landmarks.
3. Choose Open Google Maps for directions, or choose phone, email, LINE, WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram.

## Review stay information

1. Open Location & Information.
2. Read arrival, registration and payment details.
3. Expand a policy heading to read its details; native disclosure controls work by keyboard and without JavaScript.

## Navigate on mobile

1. Open the menu control in the header.
2. Choose a page or the contact section.
3. Use the language controls, gallery controls and contact links as normal touch targets.
