# Text Wireframes

These outlines describe the delivered page hierarchy; they are not pixel measurements.

## Shared shell

**Wide screen:** dark branded header with logo, four-page navigation, TH/EN control and contact action. **Narrow screen:** logo, language control and disclosure menu. All pages end with contact/social links, secondary navigation and a back-to-top link.

## Home

1. Wide hotel-photo hero with brand headline and actions for rooms, contact and directions.
2. Introductory hotel copy and key highlights.
3. Room preview cards linking to Rooms.
4. Facility preview and location preview.
5. Contact call-to-action and shared footer.

## Rooms

1. Page heading and short introduction.
2. Room category navigation/chips.
3. Five room cards with photo, room name, capacity, bed, amenities and inquiry link.
4. Related photo gallery with lightbox controls.
5. Shared footer.

## Facilities

1. Page heading and short introduction.
2. Grouped room amenities and shared hotel services, with supporting photos where useful.
3. Local transport service information.
4. Shared footer.

## Location & Information

1. Address, nearby landmarks, hotel photo and map action.
2. Contact channel cards.
3. Check-in/out, registration and payment information.
4. Expandable policy details.
5. Shared footer.

On tablet and mobile, multi-column sections wrap into fewer columns and then one column. Content remains in normal document flow; no text area relies on a fixed height.
